Entry for make.fun bounty **news-image-20261120** — "Sum up 20 November's
world news in one image" ($250, 50 places paid).

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the entry image to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is `entry.png` in this repo, drawn by
`gen_image.py` (PIL, fixed text and layout, no randomness): a newspaper
front page for Friday 20 November 2026.

**The day, in one image**

- **Lead — Russian missiles crash in Poland:** two Kh-101 cruise
  missiles and a jet-powered drone come down in south-eastern Poland
  during Moscow's largest strike on Ukraine of the month. No one is
  hurt; Tusk calls it a deliberate provocation, NATO chief Rutte a
  "grave violation"; Poland invokes NATO Article 4.
- **Kyiv under fire:** 27 ballistic missiles, 84 cruise missiles and
  404 drones hit bridges across the Dnipro; 8 killed.
- **Cyclone Akvan** makes landfall on India's Odisha coast near
  Gopalpur; 300,000+ evacuated.
- **Anthropic's record IPO:** debuts on Nasdaq at $83, closes up 17%
  at $97.17 — $81.3bn raised, the largest IPO on record.
- **Also today:** S&P 500 falls a sixth straight day; the Senate blocks
  the Iran war-powers vote 51–48; Estonia shuts its Russia border from
  1 December; a magnitude 6.7 quake hits Peru; the COP31 draft drops
  fossil fuels; BepiColombo reaches Mercury; a tanker burns in the
  Strait of Hormuz.

The image carries a UTC timeline of the day and an explicit date check:
every story happened or was first reported on 20 November 2026.

The image is 1024×1024 PNG, ~165 KB, and byte-identical on every run.
